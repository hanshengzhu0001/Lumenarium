What we optimize for

For production-facing scene generation, system-level usefulness matters more than optimizing every isolated pose coordinate.

PRIMARY PRIORITIES
  1. Asset completeness
  2. End-to-end speed
  3. Low interpenetration
  4. Physical and semantic relations between objects
ACCEPTED TRADE-OFFS
  1. Exact pose of an individual retrieved asset
  2. Small, non-critical unsupported details
DeepSearch improves retrieval speed and coverage, but its pose operating point can reduce per-object rotation/translation accuracy.

Why it is faster—and more physical

SCENELM

Optimize only what is implicated

Relation Programs compile support, collision, plane, and semantic constraints into explicit factors. SceneLM updates only the objects and degrees of freedom touched by violated relations, then applies exact leaf-translation Schur elimination where safe.

global SA-5000 → scoped 400-step relation optimization

Less blind search: S4 drops from 677.8 s to 192.9 s.

SCENEPROOF

A proposal is not a commit

Every candidate must carry mesh collision, support/contact, COM and family-level non-regression evidence. Failed candidates restore the incumbent; missing evidence becomes unresolved instead of silent success.

accept(Δ) ⇔ local gates ∧ family non-regression
